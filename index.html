Flow Dots

Flow Dots is a glowing connect-the-dots puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Draw pipes to join every pair of matching dots and fill the whole board.

Frequently asked

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I get more hints?
You start with 3 hints and receive one free hint every day.

A level seems impossible.
Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and every cell must be filled. A hint reveals one pipe.
