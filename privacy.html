Privacy Policy

Flow Dots · Effective date: October 13, 2026 · Türkçe

Flow Dots ("the game") is developed and published by YKM Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No accounts, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • We collect anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to improve the game.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Information we collect

We use services from Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics records gameplay events such as which level was started or finished, how long it took, whether a hint was used, and tutorial progress, together with general device information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, country) and a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase. This identifier is not linked to your identity.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report (technical details of the error and device state) if the game stops unexpectedly.

We use this information only to understand how the game is played, find difficult levels, and fix bugs. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the game.

Google processes this data on our behalf and may store it on servers outside your country, including in the United States. See Google's privacy policy and Firebase privacy information.

Information that stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, settings, language choice and hint balance are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example when new daily puzzles are ready) directly on your device. No data is sent to us for this. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

Ads and tracking

The current version shows no ads and does not track you. If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and on iPhone you will be asked for permission before any tracking.

Data retention

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, after which they are deleted automatically.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, to object to its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the data we receive is not linked to your identity, the simplest way to stop all collection and reset the identifier is to delete the app. For any request, email us at the address below.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page before they take effect.
