Privacy Policy

Flow Dots · Last updated: October 12, 2026

Flow Dots ("the game") is developed by Kaan Akyazi. This policy explains what information the game uses.

What we collect

Flow Dots has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, contacts or location.

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, we use Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics records anonymous gameplay events, for example which level was started or finished, how long it took and whether a hint was used, together with general device information (model, OS version, country).
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report if the game stops unexpectedly.

This data is not linked to your identity, is not sold, and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, settings and hint balance are saved on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Ads

The current version shows no ads. If a future version adds ads, this page will be updated before that version is released, and on iPhone you will be asked for permission before any tracking.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that use data (for example ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new date above.
