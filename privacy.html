Privacy Policy

Flow Dots · Last updated: October 8, 2026

Flow Dots ("the game") is developed by Kaan Akyazi. This policy explains what information the game uses.

We don't collect your data

Flow Dots does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, settings and hint balance are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that use data (for example ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue.
